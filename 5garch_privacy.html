Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 07, 2026

Cybaz Solutions LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the 5G Architect mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We are committed to minimizing data collection. For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

Location Data

Certain features of the App, specifically the Satellite Tracker, require access to your device's precise location (GPS data) to function correctly. This data is used exclusively to calculate real-time Slant Range, UE Timing Advance, and Doppler Shift relative to your position.

  • Local Processing: Your location data is processed entirely locally on your device.
  • No Transmission: We do not transmit, store, or share your location data to our servers or any third-party servers.
  • Consent: Location access is strictly opt-in. You can enable or disable location services for the App at any time through your device's system settings.

Local Storage (Preferences & Configurations)

The App allows you to save custom configurations (such as Link Budget presets, custom dashboard layouts, and specific tool preferences). This data is saved locally on your device's internal storage using secure system preferences. We do not sync, harvest, or access these configurations remotely.

2. Third-Party Services and Subscriptions

While we do not collect personal data directly on our servers, the App does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, primarily to facilitate App distribution and premium feature unlocks (In-App Purchases/Subscriptions).

These third-party service providers include:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: Used for managing the installation, updates, and secure payment processing for "Premium" features. We do not have access to your personal payment details (such as credit card numbers). We only receive an anonymous digital receipt verifying your purchase status.

Please refer to the respective privacy policies of Apple and Google for details on how they handle your data.

3. Log Data and Analytics

We want to inform you that whenever you use our App, in the case of an error in the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

This data is completely anonymized and is used solely to identify, debug, and fix crashes to improve the App's stability.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Since your data (such as Link Budget presets and precise location calculations) remains on your local device, the security of that data relies heavily on your device's operating system safeguards. We recommend utilizing device-level security (passcodes, biometrics) to protect your local data.
