About Cybaz Solutions

Built for a better Ember mug experience.

Mug Monitor is a hobby project shaped by everyday coffee, practical needs, and feedback from other Ember owners.

Why Mug Monitor exists

Mug Monitor began with a simple goal: help a mug reconnect reliably after leaving home and coming back, while keeping temperature and battery information visible without repeatedly opening an app. That small experiment grew into background monitoring, Ready to Drink alerts, Apple Watch Live Activities, a Wear OS companion app, and more.

Cybaz Solutions maintains Mug Monitor as a hobby project, answers support messages, and keeps improving it when a rough edge appears or a user sends a good idea.

Technical background

5G and LTE engineering

The technical background behind Cybaz Solutions includes cellular systems, RF engineering, and software tools for 5G and LTE work. This site currently focuses on Mug Monitor rather than consulting services.

Independence

A third-party app

Mug Monitor is developed independently and is not affiliated with Ember Technologies. The app cannot replace the manufacturer for firmware, hardware repairs, replacement parts, or warranty support.
