About the developer

I built the Ember app I wanted to use.

I’m Matt Hunsaker. Mug Monitor is a personal hobby project shaped by my own morning coffee and the feedback of other Ember owners.

Why Mug Monitor exists

I wanted my mug to reconnect reliably after I left home and came back. I also wanted the temperature and battery where I could see them without repeatedly opening an app. That small experiment grew into background monitoring, Ready to Drink alerts, Apple Watch Live Activities, a Wear OS companion app, and more.

I maintain Mug Monitor outside my regular work. I use the app myself, answer support messages, and keep improving it when I find a rough edge or a user sends a good idea.

My day job

5G and LTE engineering

My professional background is in cellular systems, RF engineering, and software tools for 5G and LTE work. I’m currently busy with that work, so this site now focuses on Mug Monitor rather than consulting services.

Independence

A third-party app

Mug Monitor is developed independently. I am not affiliated with Ember Technologies, and the app cannot replace the manufacturer for firmware, hardware repairs, replacement parts, or warranty support.
