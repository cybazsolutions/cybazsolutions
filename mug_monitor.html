Mug Monitor

The advanced companion app for your Ember mug. Get reliable background monitoring, persistent notifications, and visual pulsing alerts.

How It Started

Mug Monitor started as a hobby project. I wanted really good connectivity—a connection that would last all day AND I wanted to quickly see the temperature/status without having to open the app (silent notifications)! I also made sure the mug would seamlessly re-connect if I left the house and came back. After a few months of working on the Android app, everything was working exactly how I liked it. I even added a bonus feature to pulse my mug LED color when the temp was ready (to quickly catch my eye). But then, my iOS family members wanted a similar experience... so I spent several more months getting it working for them as well. Now, it's available for everyone who takes their hot beverages seriously.

Smart Monitoring

Visualize your drink's temperature instantly without opening the app. Through silent notifications and lock screen updates, Mug Monitor ensures your coffee or tea is never too hot or too cold.

Reliable Connectivity

Engineered for persistence, the app actively handles Bluetooth reconnections and strives to maintain a long-term connection, ensuring you stay linked to your mug throughout the day.

Visual Pulsing

Mug Monitor pulses your mug's LED in your personalized color when your drink is ready. See status at a glance—it helps visualize your drink is ready a little better than just the static white LED.

Native Experiences

Optimized for both Android and iOS.

Android

Silent Notifications

Quickly check your mug status. View live temperature and battery status directly in your notification shade.

iOS

Live Activities

Track your brew from the Lock Screen. Live Activities keep your mug's temperature front and center.
