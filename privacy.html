Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Cybaz Solutions provides this application with free features and optional paid Premium features. The service is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service on either Android or iOS platforms.

Device Permissions (Bluetooth & Location)

To ensure the functionality of the application, specifically to connect to your Ember Mug hardware, we require specific permissions on your mobile device.

"This app uses Bluetooth and/or Location Services solely to scan for and connect to your Ember Mug locally. We do not collect, store, or transmit your physical location data to any external server."

Android Requirements

Location Permission (Fine/Coarse): On the Android operating system (specifically Android 11 and lower), scanning for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices requires the "Location" permission. This is an operating system requirement because BLE beacons can theoretically be used to determine a user's location.

Nearby Devices Permission: On Android 12 and higher, we utilize the "Nearby Devices" permission to scan for the mug without needing your physical location.

iOS Requirements

Bluetooth Permission: On iOS, we explicitly request permission to access Bluetooth. This access is used exclusively to detect and communicate with your Ember Mug. iOS does not require Location services for this specific type of Bluetooth connection, and we do not request GPS access on iOS devices.

Apple Health and Caffeine Logging

On iOS, if you choose to enable caffeine logging, Mug Monitor writes the caffeine amount you select to Apple Health. Mug Monitor requests write-only access to dietary caffeine data and does not read your Apple Health data.

Caffeine entries are stored by Apple Health. Cybaz Solutions does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or use Apple Health data for advertising, analytics, or data mining. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or device Settings.

Automatic Live Activities (iOS)

Mug Monitor uses a Cloudflare-hosted relay and Apple Push Notification service to start and end Live Activities when the app is not open. When automatic Live Activities are enabled and available through Premium or an active trial, the app sends Apple-generated Live Activity tokens, the mug's display name, request identifiers, and operational timing information such as the start of an idle period and the selected timeout. Automatic Live Activities are enabled by default for eligible users unless a different choice has been saved.

This information is used to operate Live Activities, schedule their ending, protect the service, and prevent duplicate or excessive requests. These relay requests do not include detailed temperature readings, battery readings, drink history, physical location, or Apple Health data. We do not use relay information for advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale to data brokers.

Storage and Retention

The relay processes the mug's display name and push-to-start token without persisting their raw values in its database. A token needed to end a pending Live Activity is encrypted before storage and cleared when the pending operation is completed or canceled. Token hashes, request identifiers, revisions, and timing records are retained for scheduling and duplicate protection. Records older than seven days become eligible for cleanup. Cleanup occurs as requests are processed, so records may remain longer during periods without traffic.

Your Controls

You can turn off Start Live Activities Automatically in Settings to stop new automatic-start requests. Turning it off also requests cancellation of known pending idle-ending operations; cancellation requires a working internet connection. Previously retained operational records remain subject to the retention process above. Muting automatic-start failure alerts changes notifications only and does not disable the relay feature.

Information Collection and Use

Mug settings, detailed mug sensor readings, and drink history are processed on your device. Automatic Live Activities use the off-device service described above. If you contact us for support, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request.

Apple, Google Play Services (on Android), and Cloudflare provide platform, delivery, hosting, and security services. Our public website is hosted using GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Connections to these services involve standard network information, such as an IP address, request time, and technical connection details. Providers process service traffic under their applicable privacy and security practices.

Privacy policies for these service providers:

Diagnostics and Service Logs

Diagnostic information may be stored locally on your device or shared with us if you choose to send it in a support request. The Live Activity relay does not write raw tokens or mug display names to application logs. Stored Cloudflare Workers application logs and traces are disabled for the relay. This does not mean that Cloudflare, Apple, or our website host retains no network, security, or operational information; their handling is governed by their applicable privacy and security practices.

Security

But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
