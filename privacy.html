Device Permissions (Bluetooth & Location)

To ensure the functionality of the application, specifically to connect to your Ember Mug hardware, we require specific permissions on your mobile device.

"This app uses Bluetooth and/or Location Services solely to scan for and connect to your Ember Mug locally. We do not collect, store, or transmit your physical location data to any external server."

Android Requirements

Location Permission (Fine/Coarse): On the Android operating system (specifically Android 11 and lower), scanning for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices requires the "Location" permission. This is an operating system requirement because BLE beacons can theoretically be used to determine a user's location.

Nearby Devices Permission: On Android 12 and higher, we utilize the "Nearby Devices" permission to scan for the mug without needing your physical location.

iOS Requirements

Bluetooth Permission: On iOS, we explicitly request permission to access Bluetooth. This access is used exclusively to detect and communicate with your Ember Mug. iOS does not require Location services for this specific type of Bluetooth connection, and we do not request GPS access on iOS devices.
